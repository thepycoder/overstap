# Web UI on overstap data — 2026-11-05

`overstap serve` + `web/` (lane timetable ported from bike-transit), MOTIS v2.11.4 with
the 2026-10-29 import, NMBS + De Lijn RT, six GBFS feeds incl. generated Hoppy Kempen.
Tested in Playwright on 390×844 (touch) and 1280×800.

## Response times (cloud box, debug build)

| Tab | MOTIS calls | Time | Payload |
|---|---|---|---|
| Folding bike | 2 plans (18 and 25 km/h) | 1.2 s | 78 KB, 19 options |
| Shared bikes | 2 plans + hub/tour street routes | 4.7 s | 145 KB, 24 options |
| On foot | 1 plan | 0.5 s | 54 KB, 9 options |

(Lokeren Hillarestraat → Nekkerhal, Fri 08:00.)

## What it shows

- **Folding, leave now (Thu 07:52)**: live data in the first answer: IC 07:49 Lokeren
  6 min late; the bike leg is moved to meet it, the change at Berchem still holds, arrival
  unchanged → "On time".
- **Shared, Retie → Antwerpen-C, 20:01**: Hoppy e-bike 20:21 → Geel (43′ at 20 km/h),
  IC 21:06 → 21:52, the same arrival as bus 47 20:34 + IC 20:58. This is the option the
  rider missed when bus 47 was late.
- **Shared, Lokeren → Nekkerhal**: Blue-bike round trip from Mechelen station and
  Donkey via hub side by side; walking variants of the same trains drop behind "+N slower".
- **Arrive by 09:00**: ★ on the latest departure that makes it (08:02 → 08:53).

## Fixed on the way

- Leg shapes were empty: MOTIS fills `legGeometry` only with `detailedLegs=true`.
- MOTIS's change between two trains is a WALK leg; the detail view folds it into the gap.
- Blue-bike tours that save nothing (destination next to the station) are no longer
  offered: a tour must beat walking by ≥ 3 min.
- `motis server` reads `data/config.yml`: Hoppy had to be added there to load.

## Gaps

- NMBS GTFS has no tracks; MOTIS shows one only when RT assigns a platform.
- NMBS has no shapes: trains are straight lines between stops on the map.
- De Lijn RT match rate fell to 75 % (static feed is a week old): nightly re-import needed.
- Rental e-bikes are searched at 15 km/h (ADR-013).
- The bike leg before a train is drawn ending exactly at departure ("Just in time");
  a boarding buffer for the folding bike is not modelled yet.
