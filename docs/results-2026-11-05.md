# Results 2026-11-05 — shared-mobility coverage (TODO-sharing A0)

Fetched from home (Victor's laptop); the cloud workspace could reach only GitHub.
Measured with `tools/hub_coverage.py`: a hub offer counts as covered when a configured
feed of that operator has a station or parked vehicle within 250 m of the hub centroid.
**MOTIS import of the new feeds is not tested yet.**

## Hoppinpunt dataset

- 961 hubs in 15 vervoerregio's: Leuven 129, Limburg 110, Vlaamse Rand 85, Kempen 81,
  Antwerpen 79, Gent 74, Dender 67, Vlaamse Ardennen 64, Mechelen 55, Midwest 45,
  Westhoek 43, Brugge 43, Waasland 38, Kortrijk 31, Oostende 17. The press says "Aalst";
  the data says "Dender".
- `Status`: 276 `Actief`, 685 `Mobiliteitsknooppunt`. Both kinds carry real offers (most
  of the 78 Hoppy Kempen zones are `Mobiliteitsknooppunt`), so don't filter on it.
- 746 bike, cargo-bike and step offers. Operators (hubs): Hoppy 244, Blue-bike 216,
  Donkey 142, Nextbike 66, Mobit 44, Bird 15, Velo 10, Lime 7, Villo 1. The other
  operators listed are car sharing (Cambio 122, Mobilize Share 112, Claus2you 63, …).
- Some entries are stale. Bird is listed at 15 hubs while its Antwerp feed has 0
  vehicles. Lime is listed at 7 Antwerp hubs, but its Antwerp feed returns 404.
